    What the numbers were hiding

    Conversion tracking

    Google reported ₹12.56 crore of conversion value in August. The whole company — every channel — took ₹1.07 crore. Most of the engagement's analysis went into finding out which numbers could be trusted.

    11.8×

    Google claimed 11.8 times the company's revenue. Stand_14Jan booked ₹12.33 Cr between 1 and 4 August — 99.7% of its month — on ₹53,896 of spend.

    A tracking break nobody logged · tracked checkouts as a share of real orders

    Coverage fell from 41.9% in December to 2.9% in February, sat at 2–4% until May, and reached 57.0% in August. Orders barely moved. Every conversion-rate trend between February and May is void, and August's checkout "improvement" cannot be separated from the tracking recovering.

    The funnel Shopify can see · August

    Shopify reports a 0.01% conversion rate: it counted 68 checkouts against 1,960 orders, because 96.5% complete in third-party checkouts that emit no events. The true rate is 0.466%.

    What I got wrong

    Seven calls, owned

    A case study that only lists wins is a sales page. These are the decisions and readings that were wrong, what they cost, and how each was caught.

    1

    Raised the brand budget against my own spec

    On 17 Aug I took Search | Brand | Bangalore from ₹2,000 to ₹3,100 a day. My AG04 spec, written the same day, said cap it at ₹150–200 — measured brand demand was about ₹64 a day. The surplus went into broad match buying non-brand clicks. The campaign was also on Smart Bidding, so the ₹12 ad-group caps did nothing: AG04 ran at ₹36.61.

    Cost ₹57,249 at ₹2,408 CPA vs ₹36 on Brand_3juneCaught 24 Aug bid-strategy pre-flight
    2

    Let spend scale on an unverified value signal

    August billing rose 143% while Stand_14Jan and Sales_PMax_CatchAll were reporting impossible value. CatchAll scaled 537% and Stand_14Jan 50% on numbers later shown to be defective. Only S_Tofu's scale-up was sound — its CPC fell to ₹4.67 on a 513% click increase.

    Cost MER 19.61× → 9.82×Caught 1 Sep, daily per-campaign exports
    3

    Carried a false P0 through four documents

    "33 lifetime add-to-cart events, purchase tracking broken" came from the original audit and went into the Stage 4 plans, the C04 walkthrough and the blended review. It was read off goal cards that only count primary actions. Add-to-cart fired 26,737 times. A Shopify pixel rebuild and a 7 Sep scaling hard-stop were both built on it.

    Cost A week of gated decisionsCaught 2 Sep, reading the conversion actions directly
    4

    Misread a 100-row sample as a broken export

    The top-100 search terms by cost showed zero conversions on every row. I read it as a bad export and drafted a blanket competitor exclusion. The full 5,05,231-term file showed those 70 competitor terms returning 112.5 conversions at ₹87 — among the cheapest in the account.

    Cost None — reversed before it shippedCaught 1 Sep, full export
    5

    Linear-scaled a partial month

    The first blended review scaled 17 days of July by 31/17. Returns post in one batch late in the month, so the first 17 days carry 66% of revenue, not 55%. July MER went out as 3.20× and was restated to 2.68× the same day.

    Cost One wrong headline, restated in publicRule since Never linear-scale a month here
    6

    Dated the festive season wrong

    The Festive & Gifting spec put Dhanteras on 26 Oct and Diwali on 28 Oct 2026. They fall on 6 and 8 November. The decision held — build early — but the runway was ten days longer than the spec claimed.

    Cost None to spendCaught 27 Aug dashboard cross-check
    7

    Left remarketing running without a stop rule

    C04 Demand Gen remarketing went live with a cart audience and three ads. By 2 Sep: ₹11,971, 3,118 clicks, zero purchases, and a second ad group already specced on the same foundation. The 92 view-through conversions argued for patience; the missing stop-or-continue date did not.

    Cost ₹11,971Open at close All-conv check pending
    Working rules that came out of it

    Read the source, not the summary card. Conversion actions, not goal tiles. Full exports, not samples.

    Verify the signal before scaling on it. Reconcile Google purchases to Shopify orders weekly; stop if Google claims more than 60%.

    Restate in public. Every correction above is written into the document it corrects. Nothing silently fixed.
